How this works Every show's expected occupancy is min(1, (demand/10) × time-of-day factor) — morning ×0.60, afternoon ×0.85, evening/prime ×1.15, late night ×0.75. Expected revenue is capacity × occupancy × price × screen multiplier. The baseline rotates movies onto each screen back-to-back in a fixed order. A movie's daily audience budget is its demand score × 100; both schedules deduct expected viewers after each accepted show. The greedy method repeatedly ranks feasible (movie, screen, start-time) candidates by current revenue per occupied minute, accepting the best one without overlap. This is a model assumption, not observed daily ticket demand. It mirrors the C++ engine; greedy does not guarantee the highest possible total revenue.
